# Privacy Policy — Wishlist Grid View for PriceCharting

**Effective date:** November 15, 2026

Wishlist Grid View for PriceCharting ("the extension") is a free Chrome extension that improves the wishlist page on PriceCharting.com. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by PriceCharting.

**In short:** the extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any of your data. Everything it does happens in your own browser, on PriceCharting's own wishlist page.

## Where the extension runs

The extension only runs on PriceCharting wishlist pages (`https://www.pricecharting.com/wishlist*`). It requests no browser permissions and does nothing on any other website.

## What it reads

To sort, total, search and display your wishlist, the extension reads the wishlist page you are viewing: card names, sets, prices, images and the IDs PriceCharting uses for each wishlist entry. This information stays in your browser. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else.

The extension also reads your PriceCharting "default condition" setting that PriceCharting's own site saves in your browser. It is used only when you choose to move a card to your collection.

## What it stores

The extension saves a small amount of information in your browser's local storage for pricecharting.com, so your choices survive a page reload:

- whether you prefer grid view or list view
- the cards you have marked with Select or Exclude (stored as PriceCharting's wishlist entry IDs) and which of the two modes is active

This data never leaves your browser. You can remove it at any time by clicking **Clear** in the extension's Select/Exclude panel, or by clearing your browser's site data for pricecharting.com.

## Network requests

The extension makes requests only to PriceCharting.com, using your existing PriceCharting sign-in, and only for these purposes:

- **Loading your full wishlist.** PriceCharting shows the wishlist 200 items at a time. The extension requests the remaining pages from PriceCharting so it can show and sort the whole list.
- **Larger card images in grid view.** In grid view the extension loads larger versions of the card images PriceCharting already shows on the page, from PriceCharting's image servers.
- **Move to collection (only when you click it and confirm).** The extension asks PriceCharting to add the card to your collection, then uses PriceCharting's own Remove button to remove it from your wishlist.

The extension does not contact any other server. It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.

## Data sharing and sale

The developer does not receive any of your data. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to the extension's features.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle any personal information about them.
